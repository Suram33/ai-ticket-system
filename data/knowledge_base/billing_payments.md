# Billing and Payments

## Duplicate or double charges
When two identical charges appear, our support team verifies the transactions in the billing system. Sometimes one of the charges is a temporary authorization hold that disappears automatically within 3 to 5 business days. If a duplicate charge is confirmed, the extra payment is refunded to the original payment method. Refunds for duplicate charges usually appear on the customer's statement within 5 to 10 business days, depending on the bank.

## Declined or failed payments
Payments can be declined because of an expired card, an incorrect billing address, insufficient funds, or a security block by the customer's bank. Customers should update their payment method details in Account Settings under Billing, confirm that the billing address matches their bank records, and then retry the payment. If the payment still fails, customers should contact their bank or try a different payment method. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal.

## Invoices and receipts
Invoices for all payments are available on the Billing History page in Account Settings. Each invoice can be downloaded as a PDF. For business purchases, customers can add a company name and tax ID in the billing profile before downloading, and the details will appear on the invoice. Invoices are also emailed automatically after every successful payment.
