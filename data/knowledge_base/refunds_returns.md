# Refunds and Returns

## Refund policy
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase. To request a refund, go to the Orders page, select the order and choose Request Refund. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Items must be unused and in their original packaging unless the items were damaged or defective. Requests made after 30 days are not eligible for a standard refund.

## Returning an item
To return an item, customers should open the Orders page, select the order and choose Start Return. A prepaid return label is emailed within 24 hours. Items must be returned within 30 days of delivery. If the wrong item was sent, the return is free and the correct item is shipped as soon as the return is scanned by the carrier. For exchanges such as a different size, customers can choose Exchange instead of Refund when starting the return.

## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged or defective, customers should reply to the order confirmation email or contact support with photos of the item and the packaging within 7 days of delivery. Once the photos are reviewed, we send a free replacement or issue a full refund, whichever the customer prefers. Customers do not need to return damaged items unless support asks them to.
