# Subscriptions

## Cancelling a subscription
Customers can cancel a subscription at any time from the Billing section of Account Settings by selecting Cancel Subscription. After cancelling, the plan stays active until the end of the current billing period and no further charges are made. Cancelling turns off automatic renewal. Customers can resubscribe at any time.

## Subscription renewals
Subscriptions renew automatically on the renewal date shown in the Billing section. A reminder email is sent 7 days before an annual renewal. If a renewal payment fails, we retry the payment over 7 days and send an email asking the customer to update their payment method.
