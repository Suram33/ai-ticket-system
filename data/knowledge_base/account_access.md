# Account Access and Security

## Resetting a forgotten password
Customers can reset their password using the Forgot Password option on the login page. A password reset link will be sent to their registered email address. The reset link is valid for 60 minutes. If the email is not received, customers should check their spam folder or contact support. After resetting, customers should sign in with the new password on all devices.

## Temporary account lock
For security, an account is temporarily locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts. The lock is removed automatically after 30 minutes. If customers cannot wait, they can unlock the account immediately by verifying their identity through the link in the security alert email. For security, a locked account is never unlocked without identity verification.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA) problems
If a customer cannot receive or use their two-factor authentication code, they can sign in with one of the 10 backup codes provided when 2FA was turned on. Each backup code works once. If the customer has no backup codes, for example after losing their phone, they must complete identity verification with support, after which 2FA is reset and can be set up again on the new device. SMS codes can take up to 2 minutes to arrive; customers should make sure their phone has signal and that the phone number on the account is current.

## Changing the account email address
Customers can change their email address in Account Settings under Profile. A verification link is sent to the new email address and the change takes effect only after the link is clicked. A notice is also sent to the old email address for security. If the customer no longer has access to the old email, they can still complete the change as long as they are signed in.

## Deleting or closing an account
Customers can request permanent account deletion from the Privacy settings page by selecting Delete Account. Deletion is processed within 14 days, and the customer receives a confirmation email when it is complete. Deleted accounts cannot be restored. Customers should download any invoices or data they need before requesting deletion. Active subscriptions must be cancelled first.
