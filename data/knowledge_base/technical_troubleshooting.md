# Technical Troubleshooting

## Mobile app crashing or freezing
Most app crashes are fixed by updating to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play. If the problem continues, customers should clear the app cache (Android: Settings > Apps > our app > Storage > Clear Cache; iOS: delete and reinstall the app) and restart the phone. The app supports iOS 16 or later and Android 11 or later. If the app still crashes, customers should send the device model and app version to support.

## Website not loading or not working
If pages are blank, slow, or buttons do not respond, customers should clear the browser cache and cookies, disable browser extensions such as ad blockers, and reload the page. Supported browsers are the latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge. Customers can check the status page at status.example.com to see whether there is a known outage.

## Not receiving emails
If customers are not receiving order confirmations, password reset emails, or notifications, they should check their spam or junk folder and add support@example.com to their safe senders list or contacts. Customers should also confirm that the email address in Account Settings is correct and that email notifications are turned on under Notification Preferences.

## Exporting account data
Customers can export a copy of their data from Settings under Data and Privacy by selecting Export My Data. The export includes profile details, order history and saved preferences in a downloadable ZIP file. A download link is emailed within 48 hours and stays valid for 7 days.
