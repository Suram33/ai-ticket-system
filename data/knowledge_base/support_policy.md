# Support Policy

## Response times
Support is available Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 20:00, and Saturday 9:00 to 17:00. We aim to respond to high priority tickets within 4 hours and all other tickets within 1 business day.

## Escalation
If a self-service step does not solve the problem, the ticket is escalated to a support specialist who will contact the customer directly. Customers should never share their full card number or password with support.
