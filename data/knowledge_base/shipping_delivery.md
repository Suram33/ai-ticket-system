# Shipping and Delivery

## Tracking an order
A tracking link is sent by email as soon as the order ships, and it is also available on the Orders page. Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days and express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days. Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to update after shipment. If an order has not arrived 3 business days after the expected delivery date, customers should contact support so we can open an investigation with the carrier.

## Changing the shipping address
The shipping address can be changed from the Orders page by selecting Edit Address, but only before the order has shipped. Once the order has shipped, the address cannot be changed by us; customers can use the carrier's tracking page to request a redirect where the carrier offers it.

## Shipping regions
We currently ship to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and the European Union. Customers are responsible for customs duties on international orders.
